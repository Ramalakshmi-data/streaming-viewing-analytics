Viewing Analytics: Watch Next

Audience overlap between titles, and a recommender built on it

Which shows do the same people spend their time on, and can that alone drive a useful "watch next" list? Two self-contained, interactive pages built from 18,000 viewing records across 2,000 subscribers and 9 titles.

Method

Viewing time
Minutes = episodes watched × episode length, per subscriber per title.
Correlation
Pearson r between titles across the 2,000 subscribers.
Groups
Average-linkage clustering, merging while the average r ≥ 0.4. Gives four audience groups.
Match score
Strongest r between a candidate and any title you've watched, × 100, floored at 0.
